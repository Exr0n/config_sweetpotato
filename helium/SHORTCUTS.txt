Command-Shift-K opens a centered overlay on normal webpages.
Control-Q opens the MRU preview. Hold Control, tap Q to cycle, release Control to switch.
Control-Shift-Q cycles backward. Escape cancels.

Two Helium-only Karabiner rules are enabled in the selected profile.
Command-T sends Command-Shift-K, and Control-Tab sends Control-Q.
Optional Shift is preserved for reverse cycling.
Rules have separate Dvorak and default physical-key mappings.
Source asset helium/arc-shortcuts.karabiner.json is also installed at
karabiner/assets/complex_modifications/helium-arc.json.
